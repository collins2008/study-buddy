## 4. Key Conceptual Domains Verified

1. **Foundational Entrepreneurship & Core Mindset**:
   - The 5-Step Value Creation Loop: Spot $\to$ Mobilise $\to$ Test $\to$ Act $\to$ Create Value.
   - Entrepreneur (independent founder, bears total financial/legal liability) vs Intrapreneur (corporate innovator, corporate capital backing, corporate salary, no equity ownership).
   - Entrepreneur vs Traditional Manager (opportunity-driven vs resource-preservation driven; calculated risk vs variance control).
   - Invention (novel discovery, turns cash into knowledge) vs Innovation (commercialization, turns knowledge into value and cash).
   - Calculated Risk (probability estimation, feasibility analysis, contingency planning) vs Gambling (pure unmitigated chance).
2. **Forms & Sources of Innovation**:
   - 5 Innovation Types: Product, Process, Service, Marketing, Business Model (e.g., Pay-As-You-Store solar refrigeration).
   - Degrees of Novelty: Incremental ($\sim 85\%$ of innovations) vs Radical/Breakthrough.
   - Clayton Christensen's Disruptive Innovation: Simpler, lower-cost solutions targeting non-consumers that migrate upmarket to displace incumbents (e.g. POS agency banking).
   - Peter Drucker's 7 Sources of Innovation.
3. **Theories of Entrepreneurship**:
   - Joseph Schumpeter: Five New Combinations and Creative Destruction (dynamic destruction of obsolete monopolies).
   - Frank H. Knight: Risk (measurable, plannable, insurable) vs Uncertainty (unmeasurable, non-insurable). Profit as reward for bearing uncertainty.
   - Israel Kirzner: Entrepreneurial Alertness and Arbitrage (market equilibration).
   - Harvey Leibenstein: X-Inefficiency (internal operational slack) and the entrepreneur as gap-filler and input-completer.
   - David McClelland: Need for Achievement ($n\text{-Ach}$), moderate calculated risk-taking, feedback demand.
   - Saras Sarasvathy: Effectuation (Bird in Hand, Affordable Loss, Crazy Quilt, Lemonade, Pilot in the Plane).
4. **Opportunity Assessment & Nigerian Enterprise Context**:
   - Barney's VRIO Framework: Valuable, Rare, Inimitable, Organized.
   - Feasibility Analysis: Industry/Market, Product/Service, Organizational, Financial (Unit economics, Break-even point).
   - Companies and Allied Matters Act (CAMA 2020): Business Name (Sole Proprietorship) vs Private Limited Company (Ltd, separate legal entity).
   - Nigerian Startup Act 2022 and Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) compliance.
