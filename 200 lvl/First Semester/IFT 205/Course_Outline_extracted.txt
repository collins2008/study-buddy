=== SLIDE 3 ===
Course Description
Introduces the foundations, scope and evolution of Information Technology.
Explores hardware, software, networking, Internet technologies and information management.
Examines how IT is applied in education, banking, healthcare, government and business.
Introduces security, privacy, policy, ethical and social issues surrounding IT.
Provides an overview of emerging areas such as data mining, visualisation, NLP and Blockchain.
IFT 205 • Introduction to Information Technology    |    3
=== SLIDE 4 ===
Learning Outcomes
Explain the history and development of Information Technology.
Describe major domains where IT is applied.
Identify IT and related disciplines such as networking, information management, programming and web technologies.
Analyse basic security, privacy, policy and social issues associated with IT.
Compare fundamental structures of computer networks and the Internet.

=== SLIDE 7 ===
Recommended Teaching Approach
Explain
Short, clear concept explanation.
Visualise
Use diagrams, screenshots and simple models.
Demonstrate
Show technology on a laptop/phone.
Discuss
Use Nigerian and university examples.
Apply
Give a short individual or group task.
Review
End with recap questions and a quick quiz.
