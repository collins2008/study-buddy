=== SLIDE 4 ===
Critical thinking
Definition
Examine information, question assumptions, judge with reason.
Find the real problem
Separate symptoms from causes.
Evaluate opportunities
Demand, willingness to pay, sustainability.
Decide with evidence
Use information, not assumptions.
Assess risks
Costs, challenges, alternatives.
Avoid costly mistakes
Test ideas before investing.

=== SLIDE 5 ===
Critical thinking: a campus laundry
Demand
How many students need the service?
Competition
Who already operates nearby?
Prices
What do rivals charge?
Willingness to pay
Will students pay?
Costs
Equipment, water, power, labour.
Break-even
Customers needed to cover costs.
Evaluate the opportunity before renting a shop or buying equipment.

=== SLIDE 7 ===
Reflective thinking
What did I intend?
What happened?
What worked?
What did not?
Why?
What next time?
Example: online clothing store
Many followers but few orders. Reflection shows delivery delays and no return policy worry customers. Fix: reliable delivery and clear returns.

=== SLIDE 8 ===
Design thinking: five stages
1 Empathise
Understand users
2 Define
State the problem
3 Ideate
Generate solutions
4 Prototype
Build a simple version
5 Test
Get feedback
Iterative: feedback can send you back to an earlier stage.
Example: campus meals
Interviews show the issue is price, queues and delivery. A WhatsApp ordering prototype with two vendors comes before any app.
