IFT 211 (Digital Logic Design)
Course Outline

Introduction to information representation and number systems.

1.
2. Boolean algebra and switching theory.
3. Manipulation and minimisation of completely and incompletely 

specified Boolean functions.

4. Physical properties of gates: fan-in, fan-out, propagation delay, 

timing diagrams and tri-state drivers.

5. Combinational circuits design using multiplexers, decoders, 

comparators and adders.

6. Sequential circuit analysis and design, basic flip-flops, clocking and 
timing diagrams. Registers, counters, RAMs, ROMs, PLAs, PLDs, and 
FPGAs.
